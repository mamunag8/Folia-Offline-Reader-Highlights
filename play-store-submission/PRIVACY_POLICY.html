Privacy Policy — Folia: Offline Reader & Highlights

Effective Date: October 21, 2026
Developer: kasb LLC
Application ID: com.kasbllc.folia

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Folia: Offline Reader & Highlights is engineered as a 100% serverless, local-first, ad-free utility. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, saved articles, reading history, highlights, margin notes, or analytics on any kasb LLC servers.

2. 100% On-Device Article Parsing & Storage

When you save a web URL to Folia, the webpage HTML is fetched and cleaned directly on your device. All saved articles, cached cover images, multi-color highlights, and margin notes are stored strictly locally on your device using on-device SQLite storage.

3. Native Offline Text-to-Speech (TTS)

Audio narration uses your device's built-in operating system speech synthesis engine offline. No audio or text is sent to external cloud voice servers.

4. User-Controlled Markdown & JSON Export

When you choose to export your highlights to Markdown (.md) for Obsidian or Notion, or export a full library backup (.json), the files are saved directly to your chosen local folder or shared via your device's native OS Share Sheet.
