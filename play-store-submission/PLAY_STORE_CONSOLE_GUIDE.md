Save any web article for calm, distraction-free offline reading with Folia: Offline Reader & Highlights by kasb LLC — a 100% free, serverless, local-first Read-It-Later sanctuary with built-in 15-language full article translation, multi-color highlights, margin notes, offline Text-to-Speech (TTS) audio narration, and 1-tap Markdown (.md) export for Obsidian and Notion.

Unlike cloud read-it-later apps that lock your reading archive behind monthly subscriptions, require mandatory accounts, or clutter your screen with ads, Folia runs 100% locally on your device with zero accounts, zero tracking, and zero ads.

✦ 1. ON-DEVICE AD-FREE ARTICLE PARSER (100% OFFLINE READY)
• Save Any URL Instantly: Paste any link or use smart clipboard detection to strip ads, popups, cookie banners, and sidebars right on your phone.

✦ 2. BUILT-IN 15-LANGUAGE FULL ARTICLE & BILINGUAL TRANSLATOR
• Translate Any Article or Paragraph in 1 Tap: Built-in support for 15 main global languages (English, Arabic العربية, Persian فارسی, Spanish Español, French Français, German Deutsch, Chinese 中文, Hindi हिन्दी, Russian Русский, Portuguese Português, Japanese 日本語, Turkish Türkçe, Korean 한국어, Italian Italiano, and Urdu اردو) with full LTR and RTL layout support.
• Bilingual Side-by-Side or Full Translated View: Read original paragraphs stacked alongside their translation for language learning, or switch the entire article into your target language with offline caching.

✦ 3. LITERARY PAPER & INK READING ERGONOMICS
• Three Eye-Comfort Themes: Read comfortably day or night with Warm Kindle Paper (#FAF7F2), Vintage Parchment Sepia (#F3E9D2), and Midnight OLED Ink (#111315).
• Custom Typography Suite: Switch effortlessly between Literary Serif, Clean Sans-Serif, and Technical Monospace fonts with fine-grained font size (15px–26px) and line-spacing controls.
• Exact Scroll Memory: Automatically remembers your reading progress percentage on every article.

✦ 4. FOUR-COLOR HIGHLIGHTER & SECOND BRAIN HUB
• Tactile Pastel Markers: Tap any paragraph to highlight in Yellow (Key Insight), Green (Actionable/Quote), Blue (Definition/Fact), or Purple (Question/Idea).
• Inline Margin Notes: Attach personal synthesis notes directly beneath any highlighted passage.
• Daily Rediscovery Carousel: Resurface 3 random highlights from your past reading every day so your best insights never gather dust.

✦ 5. NATIVE OFFLINE TEXT-TO-SPEECH (TTS) & 1-TAP MARKDOWN EXPORT
• Multi-Language Offline TTS Audio Listener: Listen hands-free in either the original language or your translated target language with live paragraph sync and 0.75x–2.0x speed control.
